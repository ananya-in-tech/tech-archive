# Career Resources

Practical resources to help students and early-career engineers navigate opportunities and grow as professionals.

## Topics

- Resume and CV writing
- Technical portfolios and personal websites
- Internship and graduate-role preparation
- Job search and application tracking
- Professional networking and communication
- Open-source contribution
- Technical writing and public speaking
- Behavioral interviews and workplace skills

Keep advice actionable and transparent about its context. Include dates for application timelines and eligibility information because they can change. Do not present anecdotal experiences as guaranteed hiring advice.
